# Project Charter — AI-Assisted Stack Architect (AASA)

**Problem:** Choosing an AI stack is overwhelming for founders and technical leads because existing options rely on static blog content or biased vendor advice. AASA solves this by replacing weeks of speculative research with evidence-backed recommendations grounded in 3,023 real-world AI deployments.

**One-line value:** turn "what AI should we use?" into an evidence-backed shortlist in ~2 minutes, instead of a two-week research loop. [cite: 1]

**In scope:**
- AI models, APIs, model platforms, and agent/orchestration frameworks [cite: 1]
- Illustrative monthly cost forecasting, accounting for both per-token and per-seat pricing [cite: 1]
- Traceable real-case evidence retrieved from actual deployments [cite: 1]
- A 5-field intake form capturing workflow, industry, org size, privacy, and budget [cite: 1]
- A 3-block blueprint output displaying the stack, cost, and cases [cite: 1]

**Explicitly out of scope:**
- General tech-stack advice, such as databases, hosting, or front/back-end frameworks [cite: 1]
- Live pricing feeds or automated scraping [cite: 1]
- Application or boilerplate code generation [cite: 1]
- Any claims of compliance certification [cite: 1]

**Success criteria (how we'll know it worked):**
- Trust score of ≥ 4/5 median from a micro-survey of 5-8 real users [cite: 1]
- Net value metric of ≥ 70% of users confirming the tool "replaced hours of research" [cite: 1]
- Blueprint export/copy rate of ≥ 40% [cite: 1]
- 100% compliance-rule pass rate, ensuring every recommended tool respects the privacy hard filter [cite: 1]

**Timeline:** 4 weeks [cite: 1]
- **Week 1:** Normalise data, build embeddings and pricing table, draft charter, and start recruiting 5-8 testers [cite: 1]
- **Week 2:** Develop retrieval, ranking, and cost logic; build intake form; concept test with 2-3 users [cite: 1]
- **Week 3:** Wire full pipeline, polish output, implement telemetry, and test working build with 5-8 real users [cite: 1]
- **Week 4:** Feature freeze, write limitations, analyze results, and build the 10-slide pitch [cite: 1]

**Roles:**
- **Person A (Data & Logic):** Responsible for the tool normalisation map, embeddings, hard-filter and ranking logic, cost parser, LLM prompt, and data ethics (minimisation and bias checks). [cite: 1]
- **Person B (UI & Validation):** Responsible for the Streamlit intake and output UI, telemetry, micro-survey, user recruiting and sessions, pitch deck, and testing for inclusion and clarity. [cite: 1]
