How the recommendation is made

No black box. The pipeline is deterministic filtering first, then evidence-ranked retrieval — the LLM only ever writes the summary, never invents the tools or the prices.

01Retrieve

Your workflow + industry select comparable real deployments from the curated case library. Compliance posture applies a hard filter before anything is ranked.

02Rank & price

Tool names are normalised (the library has 2,511 raw variants) and cross-referenced against a hand-built pricing table that distinguishes per-seat SaaS from per-token APIs.

03Trace

Every recommended tool links back to the real cases that used it, with their reported outcomes and original source URLs. You can verify the evidence yourself.

Known limitations (what this MVP does not do)

  • Pricing is manually curated and illustrative — it is not a live feed and may be out of date. Always confirm on the vendor's own pricing page.
  • Compliance filtering is directional. ATSA does not certify HIPAA/GDPR/SOC 2 fitness; treat it as a shortlist, not sign-off.
  • The case library skews toward enterprise productivity AI (Gemini/Workspace, Copilot), so recommendations reflect that adoption pattern.
  • Company-size usage patterns are population-level (from the Stack Overflow survey), not per-case — there is no org-size field in the case data.
